# SAID staged delivery

1. **Foundation (this branch)** — API, database schema, IMEI checksum, unverified device registration, pending-review theft reports, admin-only prototype, unit/API tests.
2. **Identity and access** — institutional auth, OIDC, MFA, role and owner authorization, audit/retention controls, signed proofs of purchase and transfers.
3. **Citizen/dealer interfaces** — SAID Protect, SAID Trade, SAID Verify, authorized intake workflow, branded user interface.
4. **Case governance** — evidence review, dispute resolution, operator approval policy, appeals, recovery case lifecycle.
5. **Operator adapter contracts** — mock/sandbox interfaces first, secure CEIR/EIR protocols only after agreements with participating providers.
6. **Intelligence** — explainable duplicate IMEI rules, controlled datasets, human review and accuracy evaluation.
7. **National-readiness** — LCA oversight design, independent security assessment, lawful data sharing and pilot evaluation.

Do not activate network blocking solely from fraud scores or unverified reports.
